Human help / የሰው ድጋፍ

Send your question to a support person. Screen sharing is optional and is not required to request help.

Enter a Telegram username, international phone number, or email address matching your selection.

Do not include passwords, one-time codes, bank details, or unnecessary sensitive information. This is not an emergency service; response times may vary.

Prefer Telegram? Open @EbaMengistuBot . You may need to press Start.

Optional meeting support / አማራጭ የስብሰባ ድጋፍ

This form sends a meeting link. It does not start screen sharing or give anyone access to your device.

  1. Create or join a meeting using a service you trust.
  2. Share the meeting link with support if you choose.
  3. In the meeting application, choose whether to share a window or screen. You can stop sharing at any time.

Close private documents and notifications before sharing. Never share passwords or security codes.

Privacy and service information

  • Questions sent to the answer service may be processed by the configured AI provider. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.
  • Browser voice availability varies. If an online speech fallback is enabled, the answer text may be sent to that speech provider, including Google.
  • Help and interview-support requests may be stored or forwarded to the configured support administrator.
  • Opening Telegram uses Telegram's service. Review the message before sending it.
  • A support reference or website session ID is not a verified Telegram identity.
  • A sent request is not a guarantee of a reply, a job placement, an interview accommodation, or notification delivery.
  • For questions about your submitted information, contact the support team through the Telegram bot.
